Tube Service gives London commuters the status of every line and live arrivals at their stations, in one simple app.

• Line status for the Tube, Elizabeth line, Overground, DLR and Trams, with good-service lines grouped so disruptions stand out.
• Planning ahead? Check this weekend's status for Saturday, Sunday or both.
• Tap a line for the full details and TfL's latest updates on X.
• Live arrivals and departures.
• Favourite your lines and arrivals boards, then drag them into the order you want.
• Find stations near you, with directions to get there.
• Plan journeys across London by Tube, rail, bus, river, bike or on foot.
• Tube, Night Tube, rail and Elizabeth line maps.

Powered by TfL Open Data. Tube Service is not affiliated with or endorsed by Transport for London.
